## What has been observed

The time between a student pressing "Submit" and seeing the result has grown
over the last weeks. Getting the next activity still answers in tens of
milliseconds, but finishing one regularly takes several seconds between 15:00
and 17:00, when most schools are using the platform at the same time.

Teachers report that students press "Submit" twice because they think the
first press did not work.

## What product asks for

- Find out where the time goes.
- Propose how to bring finishing an activity back to the same response time as
  getting the next one, and what trade-offs that implies.
- Implement what you consider reasonable within the time available and write
  down what you would do next.
