## What has been reported

Since a few weeks, some students get an error when they finish an activity. The
screen shows "something went wrong", and when they go back the activity they
just finished is offered to them again, so they have to redo it.

It does not happen to everyone, and it seems worse in the afternoon. The teacher
says it happened to eleven students in one class on Tuesday between 15:00 and
15:20. Nothing changed on their side: same devices, same network, same
itinerary as the previous weeks.

## Steps the teacher followed

1. The student opens the itinerary and gets the next activity.
2. The student answers the exercises and presses "Submit".
3. After a while, the error appears. The student goes back to the itinerary.
4. The same activity is offered again as if it had never been submitted.

## What product wants to know

- Why is this happening?
- Is student data being lost?
- What should we do about it, short term and long term?
